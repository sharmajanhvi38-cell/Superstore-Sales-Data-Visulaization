# Superstore Sales & Profit — Data Visualization Project

## Project Overview
This project transforms raw Superstore sales data into meaningful visualizations to identify trends, patterns, sales performance, and profitability.

## Objective
- Analyze sales trends over time
- Compare sales across categories and regions
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze profit performance
- Explore relationships between sales, quantity, discount, and profit
- Generate business insights for better decision-making

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Visualizations
1. Monthly Sales Trend
2. Sales Performance by Category
3. Sales Performance by Region
4. Profit Performance by Category
5. Top 10 Products by Sales
6. Sales vs Profit Analysis
7. Sub-Category Profitability
8. Correlation Heatmap

## Key Insights
The analysis highlights differences in sales and profitability across categories, regions, products, and sub-categories. It also shows that high sales do not always translate directly into high profit.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing categories and products.
- Monitor low-profit and loss-making sub-categories.
- Review discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Use regional and product-level performance to improve business decisions.

## Conclusion
The visualization provides a clear overview of business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.
